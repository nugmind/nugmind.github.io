Privacy Policy

Lexi Hunt • com.mrnugi.lexihunt

Last updated: September 7, 2026 | Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Thank you for playing Lexi Hunt ("the Game", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Mr Nugi ("Developer"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected through your use of the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and your choices and rights regarding your personal data when you play Lexi Hunt.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Lexi Hunt is built with a privacy-first mindset. Most gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device storage using Android Room Database and Shared Preferences, including:

  • Game progress, unlocked levels, and category achievements
  • Coins, hint balance, and power-up inventory
  • Daily challenge calendar streaks
  • Sound, vibration, language, and board theme settings
  • Offline scores and high score statistics

This data is stored on your device and is not transmitted to our personal servers.

B. Information Automatically Collected by Third-Party Services

When connected to the Internet, trusted third-party SDKs integrated into the App may collect certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, screen resolution, locale, and network type.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) used to deliver ads, prevent ad fraud, and cap ad frequency.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and ANR reports to improve app stability and fix bugs.
  • Ad Interactions: Ad views, impressions, and clicks.

C. Optional Account Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google Sign-In / Google Play Games, basic public profile data (name, email, avatar) is used solely to identify your account and sync leaderboard scores.

2. Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.INTERNET: To load ads via Google AdMob and sync online game events.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To check if an active connection exists before network requests.

Lexi Hunt does not access your camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or external files.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Services

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Local data can be erased anytime by uninstalling the app or going to Settings > Apps > Lexi Hunt > Clear Storage.

To request deletion of any cloud-associated account data, email us at support@mrnugi.com.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Play Families Policy)

Lexi Hunt is a general audience word puzzle suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Ads served comply with family-safe policies.
